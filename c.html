i’m not the only one looking for myself in this apartment.

a black bag on the back of a chair,
a nondescript sprawl of threads,
hiding in the dark from where i sit,
barely visible.

it reminds me of a person i used to be,
a person who used to live here.

and now it’s an unwanted tenant
