A tiny library in the forest, found by accident, I couldn't stop myself from coming back. The library had been built with the most beautiful wood, and everything looked untouched, the books and the floor were perfectly clean, as if the place had been deserted for just a few days. I didn't understand why I couldn't find it on the map, or any record of it existing, but I had to go back. There was a strange looking 4 digit lock in the door, but it was unlocked. I wonder how it works and what it is supposed to protect. The first time I found the place I had been hiking for hours, following a beautiful trail in the forest. For some reason I'd been distracted by something and deviated from the path, and then I saw it. It looked like a tiny house, with a big window on the front, and a garden full of flowers. The doors were wide open, but there was no sign of life, so I decided to go in. There were bookshelves on every wall, and it was full of books. Many of them were written in latin, and there were a few I hadn't ever seen, written in a language I couldn't understand, but the ones I could understand seemed to have a pattern. There was nothing modern, I couldn't find any book published after 1970, and everything seemed to be related to the forests, or to wildlife. The walls were decorated with beautiful paintings depicting trees, animals, and nature in general. I felt so calm and at ease while looking around that I lost track of time, and as soon as I realized it was dark outside, I left the place and ran back to the trail. I told my mom about the library, and how I wanted to go back, but she seemed strangely reluctant. "Don't go back, honey", she said, "you'll get lost". I found it odd that she would say that, I'd been to that trail many times and never got lost before, but I decided to ignore it. I didn't mention the library again, but I was excited about going back as soon as possible. After a few days, the library started showing up in my dreams. I could see myself, sitting comfortably in a chair, reading, but the book was blank. I would turn a page after another, but it was blank, there were no words. I was also singing while I read, the song was familiar, but I couldn't understand the words. I was singing in latin. And then I would wake up, feeling refreshed and happy. Every night I would have the same dream, and every day I would feel like going back to the place. I wanted to try and find the book I'd been reading, and I felt like I needed to sing the song I was singing in my dreams. As if something was missing in my life, and finding it would be the only way to feel whole again. For some reason I felt like I shouldn't tell anyone about this, I'm not sure why. I felt that I should go back by myself, and nobody should know where I was. That seemed like a stupid idea at first, but it kept growing in my mind until I couldn't think about anything else. So I picked a day when I knew I could be alone for the whole afternoon, and I went to the trail. I went back to the spot where I'd seen the library before, but it wasn't there anymore. I went further into the woods, and then I realized that I was lost.
