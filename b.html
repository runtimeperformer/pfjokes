the road is a river of silver and gold
flooded with storm light and haze
wet heat and drenched green
and the air is thick with the smell of hay
and tilled earth
a large black bird moves slowly over the rows
like a kite being flown by a child
that summer has only just arrived

driving through the deep center
of a nostalgic landscape
sleepy in the fragrant air passing the blooming trees
lilac and white dogwood
weedy ditches clouded by cottonwood seeds
the far off wail of a train punctuates the breeze
with a slow whistle

I am moving through a memory

a time in my life where my bare feet
were more often in the grass
than under a table

I feel nostalgia for that time
as I do for the people and places
that came and were loved
that passed and are passing
still in the stunning light of summer

the road is a river of silver and gold
the bell rang loud and unbroken
like an imperious sentence
you were gone
I picked up the phone and began to grieve
you were gone

into the summer

into the long days and nights
of youth into the cornflowers
and the goldenrod into the fields
of timothy and grass
into the stormy night where a lightning bug rose
like a planet above the grass
you were gone

into the music and the color
into the long view across the green
you were gone

into everything you were
and everything you are
everything you have been
and everything you will be
to everyone you know
you were gone

and I don't know how to stay
