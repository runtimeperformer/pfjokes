An Ongoing Puzzle

In the end, the only puzzle you will ever solve is your own. But you can learn something along the way. A flaw in any system is that you cannot see all the things you do not know. You must begin with the assumptions and expectations you have.

As you build your puzzle, you will learn what you did not know, and the puzzle will change. If you persist, you will eventually see what you cannot know, and the puzzle will seem to be no puzzle at all.

It is not a question of blind spots.
It is that your own perception is only the beginning of the game.

The greatest puzzle is that we create puzzles, both large and small, that we can never solve. We piece them together as best we can, then we build belief systems to fill in the gaps.

In this way, we can convince ourselves that we know something, but, really, what we know is only what we have imagined.

The puzzle of human existence is that we can never see all the pieces. We can only make the best sense we can with the pieces we have. It is a jigsaw puzzle we cannot solve, yet we must live with it in some way. And it seems our beliefs do not help us. They only make things worse.

The world itself is not a jigsaw puzzle.
We make it so with our beliefs and expectations.
The world as it is has no expectations. It just is.
What we are left with is the puzzle of our own minds. And that puzzle is unsolvable.

A puzzle cannot be solved with only pieces of the puzzle. The pieces must be put together in a certain way to form a picture.

But the picture is never complete.

There is always something missing, something we do not know. And so we make up stories to fill in the gaps.

These stories are our beliefs.

They are not the truth, but they are all we have.

We live in a world of stories, and we are the storytellers. We create the stories that make up our lives, and we live by them.

But these stories are not the truth.

They are only the best we can do with the pieces we have.

The world is not a puzzle to be solved.
It is a mystery to be lived.

And the only way to live it is to let go of our beliefs and expectations, and to be open to the mystery of what is.
