leaving me behind
stranded in another realm,
something is broken.
unconnected, unaware, moments fleeting,
everything passing by as I am fighting to the surface.
discombobulated, searching for the answers.
waves lapping against my shoulders, gasping for breath.
glaring faces, yelling at me, showing their teeth.
foamy words,
splashing liquid,
raising like water
from the sea of my subconscious.
like blisters on my mind.
the light faded, but the shadows remain.
they drag me beneath, screaming for help.
so much pain in my body,
my limbs torn from their sockets.
nothing left to move me through space.
don’t look me in the eye, just answer please
if I will make it out alive
